About

Goldy has been tattooing for seventeen years, and hand poking for the last of them. Every piece is made without a machine — needle, ink, and time.

She works from a quiet studio in Hawea Flat, just outside Wanaka, taking a small number of clients so that each sitting stays unhurried.

Flash and custom designs are both welcome. Bring an idea, or choose something already drawn.

Before you send

Goldy tattoos by hand, one client at a time, so dates fill up slowly and gently. A small deposit holds your booking and comes off the final price.

You'll need to be 18 or over, and well rested and fed on the day. If you're unwell, pregnant, or on blood thinners, let her know beforehand and she'll talk it through with you.

Designs are drawn for you and stay Goldy's own artwork. Aftercare notes come home with you on the day.

The details you send here are used only to answer your enquiry, and aren't shared with anyone else.
